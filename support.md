# NoteTkr Support

NoteTkr records conversations on your iPhone and turns them into transcripts, summaries and
to-dos with AI that runs on the iPhone.

**Contact:** notetkr01@gmail.com. We usually answer within two business days. In NoteTkr,
**Settings → Contact support** starts an email with your app version and build filled in.

## Send a diagnostic report

If processing failed, open the note and tap **Report this problem** below "Error details". At any
other time, use **Settings → Share diagnostic report**. NoteTkr shows exactly what the report
contains before you share it: no notes, transcripts, audio or chat text. For a failed note you can
choose to attach that note's transcript; it is off unless you turn it on. Share the report by
email to notetkr01@gmail.com and quote its Report ID in your message.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is free, and what does NoteTkr Full add?**
NoteTkr is a free download, and your first 3 new notes are free in total: recordings, imported
audio files and typed notes all count. After that, the one-time in-app purchase NoteTkr Full
unlocks unlimited new notes. There is no subscription. Every note you already have stays
available without it: you can still open, play, search, edit, chat with, export and sync it. The
library shows how many free notes you have used, and tapping that line opens the unlock screen.
A note counts only once it worked: a typed note once it is saved with text, a recording or import
once its transcript has words in it.

**Do deleted notes give back free notes?**
No. Deleting notes does not reset your free notes, even after you delete all of them. Deleting
and reinstalling NoteTkr usually keeps the count too (a copy stays in your iPhone's Keychain
and, with iCloud, in your iCloud account), though that is not guaranteed.

**How do I restore my purchase?**
NoteTkr Full belongs to the Apple Account that bought it. On a new iPhone or after reinstalling,
it usually turns on by itself; if not, tap **Restore Purchases** on the unlock screen or in
**Settings → About**, signed in with that Apple Account. Restoring never charges you again. If it
says "No NoteTkr Full purchase was found for this Apple Account.", check which Apple Account is
signed in to the App Store. If it says "Couldn't reach the App Store. Try again later.", check
your connection and try again. If your purchase is waiting for approval (Ask to Buy), NoteTkr Full
turns on by itself once it is approved. For a refund, use Apple's Report a Problem
(reportaproblem.apple.com).

**Which iPhones does NoteTkr support?**
iPhone 16 Pro, iPhone 16 Pro Max and newer: the iPhone 17 family, including iPhone Air and
iPhone 17e, and later iPhones. NoteTkr's AI runs on the iPhone, and these are the models it is
made for. iPhone 16, iPhone 16 Plus, iPhone 16e, iPhone 15 Pro and older iPhones are not
supported. NoteTkr installs on any iPhone with iOS 26, but on those it opens on one screen, "This
iPhone isn't supported", with "NoteTkr needs iPhone 16 Pro or newer. Its on-device AI can't run
on this iPhone." It shows nothing else there: no notes, no free notes, no recording and nothing
to buy. We would rather support fewer iPhones than offer an app that works only in part on other
models. Nothing is downloaded on that iPhone, so you can simply delete NoteTkr from it.

**Why does NoteTkr download about 2.5 GB?**
The AI models run on your iPhone, so they are downloaded once after you agree to the model terms
and choose Download now: about 713 MB for transcription and 1.7 GB for summaries and to-dos.
Speaker labels (32 MB) and search by meaning (212 MB) are optional. The download uses Wi-Fi unless
you allow cellular. You can record before the download; those recordings are processed when it
finishes.

**The download or processing stopped.**
Keep NoteTkr open while it downloads and processes: both pause when you leave the app and continue
when you come back. NoteTkr keeps the screen on while it works. Check free storage in
Settings → General → iPhone Storage.

**Processing failed or says there is not enough memory.**
Close other apps and tap Retry on the note. If it fails again, send a diagnostic report from the
note.

**Do my recordings leave my iPhone?**
Transcription, summaries and chat run on your iPhone; nothing is sent to us or to an AI service.
If iCloud sync is on (the default for new installations), your notes and recordings are stored in
your own private iCloud account, which we cannot access. Turn sync off in
Settings → iCloud & Backup.

**How do I delete my data?**
Delete notes (they stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days), use Delete Entire iCloud Library in
Settings → iCloud & Backup, Delete all chats in the chat history, and Clear diagnostic data on the
diagnostic report screen. Deleting the app removes its files from the iPhone; the small record of
which notes used a free note can stay in the iPhone's Keychain and in your iCloud account, so
that a reinstall keeps the count. See the [privacy policy](https://github.com/Chabasx/notetkr-legal/blob/main/privacy.md).

**Do I need permission to record people?**
Let people know before you record them. Some places require everyone's consent; you are
responsible for following the law where you record.
